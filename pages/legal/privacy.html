Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Phryco ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our video sharing platform (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways to provide and improve our Service.

Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your username and a hashed version of your password.
  • Profile Information: You may choose to provide additional information for your channel profile, such as a channel description, avatar, and banner image.
  • User Content: We collect the content you create, upload, or post to the Service. This includes your videos, comments, QnA forum posts and replies, and custom emojis.
  • Birth Date: To comply with age-related content restrictions, we ask for your birth date. This information can only be set once and is used to grant or restrict access to age-sensitive content.

Information We Collect Automatically

  • Usage Data: We collect information about your activity on the Service, such as videos you watch (watch history), videos you like, channels you subscribe to, and your interactions with advertisements.
  • Telemetry and Analytics: To improve our recommendation engine and understand content performance, we collect data such as how long you watch a video (watch time) and which parts of a video are most replayed (heatmap data).
  • Device and Connection Information: We automatically collect certain information from your device, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and user agent. We also infer your country from your IP address to assist with ad targeting and regional content analysis.
  • Cookies: We use cookies (small text files stored on your device) to maintain your login session. We do not use cookies for cross-site tracking.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To Provide and Maintain the Service: To operate our platform, stream videos, process uploads, and manage accounts.
  • To Personalize Your Experience: To recommend videos and content you might like based on your watch history and engagement.
  • To Manage the Phrybucks Economy: To process transactions, calculate creator earnings, and manage ad campaign budgets.
  • To Communicate With You: To send you important notifications about your account or content, such as when a video is approved or you receive a Phrybucks transfer.
  • To Enforce Our Policies: To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service, including content moderation and account actions.
  • To Improve the Service: To analyze usage trends and telemetry data to understand how our Service is used and identify areas for improvement.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to third-party marketers.

Information Shared Publicly

Certain information is always public on Phryco. This includes:

  • Your username, channel avatar, banner, and description.
  • Any content you upload that is set to "Public," including videos, comments, and QnA posts.
  • Your subscriber count.

Information Shared with Other Users

  • When you transfer Phrybucks to another user, they will see your username as the sender.

For Legal Reasons

We may share your information if we believe that disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to enforce our Terms of Service; or to protect the safety, rights, or property of Phryco, our users, or the public.

5. Your Choices & Rights

You have control over your information on Phryco.

  • Access and Update Information: You can review and update your channel information (description, avatar, banner) in the Phryco Studio settings.
  • Ad Preferences: You can choose to disable ads by purchasing a monthly ad-free subscription with Phrybucks from your main Account Settings page.
  • Watch History: You can view your watch history and have the option to clear it entirely.
  • Account Deletion: You can request the deletion of your account. This action is irreversible and will schedule your account and all associated content for permanent deletion from our servers.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. We implement measures such as password hashing, rate limiting, and use of secure protocols (HTTPS) to protect your information. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you choose to delete your account, your data will be permanently removed from our systems in accordance with our data deletion schedule. Content that is moved to the "trash" will be retained for a short period (1 hour) before permanent deletion to allow for restoration.

8. Children's Privacy

Phryco is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the relevant age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information from our servers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
