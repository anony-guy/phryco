Privacy & Data Governance

Phryco Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026 | Version: 2.4.0

1. Information We Collect

Phryco collects data to provide video streaming, user authentication, and monetization services:

  • Account Credentials: Username, email address, salted password hashes (bcrypt), and account roles.
  • Media & Upload Data: Uploaded video files, generated thumbnails, audio spectrogram features, WebVTT subtitles, and perceptual hashes.
  • Financial & Transaction Logs: Internal Phrybucks balance transfers, membership subscriptions, and promo code redemptions stored in our immutable ledger.
  • Security & Behavioral Telemetry: Hardware fingerprint hashes (`vaultguard_fingerprint`), linked hardware cluster IDs (`alt_cluster_id`), IP addresses, and user-agent details used by VaultGuard anti-bot defenses.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized to:

  • Deliver AES-128 token-gated HLS streams and low-bandwidth audio-only media.
  • Operate AI moderation shields (`ai_moderator.py`) to prevent spam, phishing, or unsafe content.
  • Synchronize channel memberships, creator badges, and P2P Phrybucks micro-transactions.
  • Provide optional Discord Linked Roles integration when authorized via OAuth2.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Limited data sharing occurs only for platform operations:

  • Discord Bot ("Phryco Inc."): Transmits public user IDs and role status strictly for Discord account verification and role assignment.
  • Edge Relay Nodes: Decentralized sub-relays mirror public video packages (`package.zip`) and static metadata without exposing private user session keys.

4. Cookies & Service Worker Caching

Phryco utilizes `localStorage` for JWT session tokens and Service Worker caching (`sw.js`) for offline UI resiliency. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Users may request account deletion or data exports. Account deletion soft-deletes user profiles while preserving ledger auditability for platform security.

6. Contact Us

For privacy requests or data inquiries: privacy@phryco.com
